Platform

Psychiatry workflows handled before, during, and after the visit.

Faith Health connects patient-reported information, clinical notes, symptom measures, medication history, and care-team tasks into one supervised AI workflow. The platform is designed to reduce administrative burden while giving clinicians clearer, safer context for decisions.

01

Intake Copilot

Turns patient history, screeners, prior notes, referral context, and social determinants into a clean psychiatric intake summary that clinicians can review before the first visit.

  • Structured presenting concerns and symptom history
  • Prior diagnosis, medication, therapy, and hospitalization summary
  • PHQ-9, GAD-7, sleep, substance use, and functional impact capture
  • Referral triage notes and suggested appointment preparation
02

Visit Agent

Surfaces risk signals, medication context, differential prompts, and measurement-based care tasks during the session, without taking the clinician out of control.

  • Pre-visit clinical brief with changes since last appointment
  • Medication adherence, refill gaps, side effects, and response patterns
  • Safety prompts for deterioration, suicidality, and crisis language
  • Draft assessment, plan, and follow-up tasks for clinician review
03

Care Follow-up

Checks in between appointments, escalates safety concerns, and keeps the care team informed without creating another overloaded inbox.

  • Automated symptom, mood, sleep, and adherence check-ins
  • Escalation queues for urgent or clinically meaningful responses
  • Patient education and appointment preparation messages
  • Closed-loop follow-up tasks assigned to the right care-team owner
